Katechon Technology operates the Katechon Arc platform, an AI‑driven short‑drama and video ad studio. Users can generate scripts, storyboards, video, and voiceovers from simple ideas or product briefs. The service offers planning tools, free utilities for AI filmmakers, and subscription plans starting at $12.90 per month. It aims to streamline creative production for marketers, filmmakers, and content creators through an intuitive web interface.

**Areas:** AI · Video · Creative · Platform · Automation

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Katechon Technology: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
